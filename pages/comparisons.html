Energy sources, any country vs. any country

Compares one energy source or category from one tracked grid against another - the same country, or two different ones, e.g. USA wind against Great Britain wind. Uses each country's own real stored history over a chosen time range.

Defaults to USA wind vs. Great Britain wind. Not every country tracks every source (e.g. the USA doesn't report biomass separately) - if a combination isn't available, the chart says so rather than showing a placeholder.
