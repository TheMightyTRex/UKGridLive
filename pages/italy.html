Italy: the live status of the Italian electricity grid

Italy's grid, operated by Terna, relies more heavily on gas and imported electricity than most large European grids, having phased out nuclear power entirely after a 1987 referendum. It imports significant power from France, Switzerland and the Balkans, alongside a substantial hydro fleet and a fast-growing solar sector. It isn't physically interconnected to Great Britain, so it's included here for comparison rather than as one of GB's own interconnector partners. Demand, generation, day-ahead price and generation mix below are live from the ENTSO-E Transparency Platform.

      About the Italian grid

      Terna operates Italy's transmission grid, part of the Continental Europe synchronous area. Italy phased out nuclear power entirely following a 1987 referendum, and has since relied more heavily on natural gas, hydro and imported electricity than most comparably sized European grids, alongside a fast-growing solar sector, particularly in the south.

      Italy imports significant power from France, Switzerland, Austria and Slovenia, and has no direct electrical interconnector with Great Britain. It's included on this site purely for cross-country comparison via the Comparisons page, not because of any GB grid relationship.

      Context

      Data sources for Italy

      The Elexon Insights Solution, NESO Data Portal and Carbon Intensity API that power the Great Britain pages are GB-specific and don't cover Italy, so this page draws on a different source instead:

      • ENTSO-E Transparency Platform - the pan-European grid data hub, required by EU regulation for every connected transmission system operator to publish to. Powers demand, generation, day-ahead price, the generation mix, the 24-hour mix chart and the History section above, all via Italy's own bidding zone. Requires the site operator's own free API token, obtained through a short registration process - shows "not enough recorded history yet" rather than a placeholder figure if one isn't set or ingestion hasn't run yet.

      Two figures aren't shown on this page at all: ENTSO-E doesn't publish emissions intensity (g/kWh), and its generation-per-type data doesn't include cross-border physical flow, so rather than show a permanently illustrative placeholder for either, they're left out until a real source exists. See the sitewide data sources page for how this compares to the other pages.
