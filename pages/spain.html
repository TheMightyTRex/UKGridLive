Spain: the live status of the Spanish electricity grid

Spain's grid, operated by Red Eléctrica (REE), has one of the highest wind and solar shares of any major European grid, backed by significant hydro and a shrinking nuclear fleet. It is connected to the rest of Continental Europe mainly through France, with a much smaller link to Portugal and Morocco. It is not physically interconnected to Great Britain, so it is included here for comparison rather than as one of GB's own interconnector partners.       About the Spanish grid

      Red Eléctrica de España (REE) operates Spain's transmission grid, part of the Iberian bidding zone (shared trading arrangements with Portugal) inside the Continental Europe synchronous area. Spain has built one of Europe's largest wind and solar fleets, complemented by hydro, a shrinking nuclear fleet and gas for flexible backup - giving it some of the highest renewable-generation shares of any large European grid on sunny, windy days.

      Spain's main interconnection to the rest of Continental Europe runs through France, with smaller links to Portugal and (via a subsea HVDC cable) Morocco. It has no direct electrical interconnector with Great Britain, and is included on this site purely for cross-country comparison via the Comparisons page.

      Records & context

      • Renewable share of generationRecord 56.8% in 2024 - first passed 50% in 2023
      • Installed capacitySolar PV overtook wind as the largest technology during 2024 - ~33GW wind and ~43GW grid-connected solar by 2025
      • All-time peninsular peak demand45,450MW - 17 December 2007
      • Nuclear fleet7 reactors, ~20% of generation in 2024 (19% in 2025)
      • Iberian Peninsula blackout28 April 2025, 12.33pm - grid collapse across Spain and Portugal

      Data sources for Spain

      The Elexon Insights Solution, NESO Data Portal and Carbon Intensity API that power the Great Britain pages are GB-specific and do not cover Spain, so this page draws on a different source instead:

      • ENTSO-E Transparency Platform - the pan-European grid data hub, required by EU regulation for every connected transmission system operator to publish to. Powers demand, generation, day-ahead price, the generation mix, the 24-hour mix chart and the History section above, all via Spain's own bidding zone. Requires the site operator's own free API token, obtained through a short registration process - shows "not enough recorded history yet" rather than a placeholder figure if one is not set or ingestion has not run yet.

      Two figures are not shown on this page at all: ENTSO-E does not publish emissions intensity (g/kWh), and its generation-per-type data does not include cross-border physical flow, so rather than show a permanently illustrative placeholder for either, they are left out until a real source exists. See the sitewide data sources page for how this compares to the other pages.
