Colombia's electricity grid

Colombia's grid, the Sistema Interconectado Nacional (SIN), is operated by XM and gets the large majority of its generation from hydro, backed by thermal (mostly gas) plants for dry-season reliability and a fast-growing wind and solar sector.

No live figures on this page yet - see below

About Colombia's grid

XM operates Colombia's national grid and wholesale electricity market. Hydro typically supplies around two-thirds to three-quarters of generation in a normal year, though output varies significantly with rainfall (including El Niño-driven droughts), with thermal gas plants providing backup during dry spells, and a growing wind fleet concentrated in La Guajira in the north plus an expanding solar sector.

Colombia's grid connects to both Ecuador and Venezuela, though cross-border flows with Venezuela have been intermittent in recent years due to that country's own grid reliability issues - see the Americas interconnections page.

Context

  • Hydropower share of generationTypically around 65-80% in a normal rainfall year, though this can fall sharply during El Niño droughts
  • Installed capacity mixDominated by hydro at roughly 60-65% of total capacity, with thermal (mostly gas) providing dry-season backup
  • Wind and solar growthStill a small overall share, concentrated in La Guajira in the north (wind) alongside a fast-expanding solar sector
  • Cross-border linksThe Sistema Interconectado Nacional (SIN) connects with both Ecuador and Venezuela

Sources: XM (Compañía de Expertos en Mercados) / SiMEM, IEA Colombia country profile. Last checked: 23 August 2026.

Why there are no live figures here yet

Every other country on this site (Great Britain, Ireland, the rest of Europe, the USA, Canada) has a live, keyless or free-registration API this project has actually wired up - ENTSO-E for Europe, the EIA for the USA, IESO for Ontario. Colombia's operator, XM (Compañía de Expertos en Mercados) (XM), does publish an open data portal, SiMEM - the first of its kind in Colombia's electricity sector - but this project hasn't yet built and tested a parser against it. Wiring it up would mean writing and testing a new parser against a live response, the same work that went into each of the other countries' pages - not done yet for this one.

Rather than show a fabricated number here, or nothing at all, this page instead gives real, sourced background on Colombia's grid and points directly at where the real-time data actually lives.

Where to see Colombia's live grid data

  • SiMEM (Sistema de Información del Mercado de Energía Mayorista) - Colombia's official open data portal for the wholesale electricity market, operated by XM - generation, demand, price and mix datasets.
