          Records

          The figures on this panel are real, sourced records. They are currently hand-entered and checked periodically against the sources below, rather than computed automatically from stored history - each record's "last checked" date shows when it was last verified.

          • Highest wind generation ever23,880MW - 25 March 2026, 1.30pm to 2pm
          • Wind curtailed, Q1 20263.6TWh (about 11% of potential wind output), due to transmission constraints - Montel EnAppSys estimate
          • Previous record23,825MW - 5 Dec 2025, 5.30pm

          Source: National Energy System Operator, reported by Edie and RenewableUK, March 2026; curtailment from Montel EnAppSys via Energy Voice. Last checked: 2 October 2026.

          • Highest solar generation ever15,427MW - 12 July 2026, late morning
          • Solar's share of the mix at that moment44%
          • First time above 9.5GW9,550MW - 14 May 2019
          • Different dataset, different answerCarbon Brief's analysis puts the record at ~15.2GW on 23 April 2026, with no new peak over summer 2026 - estimates of GB solar vary by source

          Source: National Energy System Operator, reported by Solar Power Portal, July 2026; NESO (2019); Carbon Brief (alternative estimate). Last checked: 2 October 2026.

          • Lowest carbon intensity reported20g/kWh - 1.30pm, 7 April 2026
          • Earlier 2026 low26g/kWh - 3pm, 25 March 2026
          • Previous headline record39g/kWh - Easter Monday, 5 April 2021
          • Average intensity, 2014 vs 2025419g → 126g/kWh

          Source: NESO monthly electricity reports for March 2026 and April 2026, Carbon Intensity API, and Carbon Brief (annual averages). Last checked: 2 October 2026.

          • Winter 2025/26 peak demand47.3GW - 5 Jan 2026, the highest since March 2018
          • Winter 2025/26 operating margin6.1GW (10.0%) - highest since 2019/20
          • Winter 2026/27, early view5.5GW (8.8%) expected margin - full Winter Outlook due October 2026

          Source: NESO Winter Outlook 2025/26, NESO winter review and NESO Winter Early View 2026/27. The "summer low" bar is a rough comparison, not a specific sourced figure. Last checked: 2 October 2026.

            Alerts

            Get a browser notification when price or carbon intensity crosses a threshold you set. This only works while this page stays open in a browser tab - this site has no server that sends push or email notifications, so closing the tab (or the browser) turns alerts off until you reopen it.

            Checked against live data roughly every 5 minutes while this page is open, plus once whenever it first loads. Your thresholds are saved in this browser only (not sent anywhere) and only fire once per crossing, so you will not get repeated notifications while a value stays past your threshold.
